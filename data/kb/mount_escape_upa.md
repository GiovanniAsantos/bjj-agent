# Mount Escape: Upa (Bridge and Roll)

## Position
Bottom player under full mount. Gi or no-gi. A fundamental escape for beginners.

## Goal
Bridge and roll the opponent over to end on top, inside their guard.

## Steps
1. Protect first: elbows tight to the body, hands defending the neck, do not push the opponent's chest with straight arms.
2. Trap one arm: grab the opponent's wrist or overhook the arm (same side you will roll to) and pin it to your chest.
3. Trap the foot on the same side by placing your foot on the outside of their foot.
4. Bridge explosively, driving the hips up and toward the shoulder of the trapped side (diagonal, not straight up).
5. Roll over the trapped side and land in the opponent's guard with good posture.

## Common mistakes
- Bridging without trapping both the arm and the foot on the same side: the opponent posts and stays on top.
- Bridging straight up instead of over the shoulder.
- Pushing the opponent with straight arms, giving the armlock.

## Defenses
- Keep a wide base, hands free to post, and float the hips with the bridge.

## Safety
Low injury risk. Do not bridge into the partner's face with the head; keep the neck safe when rolling.
