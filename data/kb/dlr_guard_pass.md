# Passing the De La Riva Guard

## Position
Top player standing against the De La Riva (DLR) guard: the bottom player hooks one leg around the outside of your lead leg, usually holding your ankle and a sleeve or collar. Mostly a gi guard.

## Goal
Neutralise the DLR hook and pass to side control or mount.

## Steps
1. Posture and grips: stand tall, keep your weight on the back leg and grip the bottom player's knees or pants to control their legs.
2. Clear the hook: turn the hooked knee inward and drop it toward the mat (or step back) to break the DLR hook.
3. Option 1 (knee cut): with the hook cleared, slide your lead knee across the bottom player's thigh while controlling their far collar or underhook.
4. Option 2 (backstep / leg drag): drag the legs to the side opposite the hook and pin them, then settle your hips on theirs.
5. Finish in side control with chest pressure and shoulder control.

## Common mistakes
- Leaning forward with weight on the hooked leg: the bottom player sweeps or takes the back (berimbolo).
- Trying to pass before breaking the grips on the ankle and sleeve.
- Passing without pinning the hips: the bottom player recovers the guard.

## Defenses (for the bottom player)
- Keep the ankle grip and use the DLR hook to off-balance before the top player clears it.

## Safety
Low submission risk, but the hooked knee can twist under pressure. Clear the hook by turning the knee, never by falling on it.
