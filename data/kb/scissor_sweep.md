# Scissor Sweep

## Position
Bottom player in closed guard, gi. The no-gi version uses wrist and overhook control instead of collar and sleeve grips.

## Goal
Off-balance the opponent and reverse the position, ending in mount.

## Steps
1. Take a deep cross collar grip with one hand (right hand on the opponent's right collar) and grip the opponent's sleeve on that same side with the other hand (left hand on their right sleeve).
2. Open the guard and shrimp slightly to the side of the sleeve grip, creating an angle.
3. Put the shin of the top leg across the opponent's belt line (knee toward their opposite shoulder) and keep the bottom leg flat on the mat behind their knee.
4. Pull the collar and sleeve toward you to bring the opponent's weight onto your shin.
5. Scissor the legs: kick with the shin and chop the bottom leg against the back of their knee. Follow the roll and finish in mount.

## Common mistakes
- Staying flat with no angle: the opponent's weight never loads onto the shin.
- Not pulling the opponent forward before scissoring.
- Letting the sleeve go: the opponent posts that arm and stops the sweep.

## Defenses
- Keep posture and weight back, base wide.
- Strip the sleeve grip and post the free arm out to the side.

## Safety
Low injury risk. Control the fall of your partner and keep the bottom leg chop at the back of the knee, never on the joint from the side.
