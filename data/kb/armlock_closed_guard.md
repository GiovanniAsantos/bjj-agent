# Armlock from Closed Guard

## Position
Bottom player in closed guard, legs locked around the opponent's waist. Gi or no-gi.

## Goal
Isolate one of the opponent's arms and hyperextend the elbow by raising the hips against it.

## Steps
1. Control one arm: grab the opponent's sleeve or wrist and pull it across your chest, keeping their elbow tight to you.
2. Put the foot on the same side as the controlled arm on the opponent's hip and use it to pivot your body, creating an angle (your head moves toward the side of the arm you are not attacking).
3. Swing the opposite leg high over the opponent's head and squeeze your knees together, pinning their arm between your thighs.
4. Keep the opponent's thumb pointing up and the arm along your centre line.
5. Raise your hips slowly while holding the wrist against your chest until the opponent taps.

## Common mistakes
- Attacking without breaking the opponent's posture first: an upright opponent stacks you and pulls the arm out.
- Not pivoting enough: without the angle, the leg cannot go over the head.
- Leaving space between the thighs: the elbow slides out.
- Cranking fast instead of raising the hips slowly.

## Defenses
- Keep posture upright and elbows inside the opponent's knees.
- Stack the attacker (drive weight forward over their head) and turn the thumb toward the mat to pull the elbow out.

## Safety
Elbow locks can injure without warning. Apply slowly, release at the first tap, and tap early when caught. Train under supervision.
