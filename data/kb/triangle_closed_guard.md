# Triangle Choke from Closed Guard

## Position
Bottom player in closed guard. Works in gi and no-gi.

## Goal
Trap the opponent's head and one arm between your legs and choke by compressing the carotid arteries.

## Steps
1. Break the opponent's posture and create a situation where one of their arms is inside your guard and the other is outside (for example, push one wrist to their hip).
2. Open the guard, put the foot of the side with the arm outside on their hip and shoot the other leg over their shoulder, behind the neck.
3. Pull the head down and lock the figure four: the back of the knee of the leg over the neck goes on the ankle of the other leg.
4. Pull the trapped arm across your body toward the side of the leg over the neck.
5. Cut an angle (turn your body perpendicular to the opponent), squeeze the knees together and pull the head down to finish.

## Common mistakes
- Locking the figure four while facing the opponent straight on: the choke is weak without the angle.
- Letting the trapped arm stay in the middle instead of pulling it across.
- Not controlling the head: the opponent postures up and escapes.

## Defenses
- Never leave one arm in and one arm out of the guard.
- Keep posture, bring the trapped arm back inside, or stack the attacker and step around toward the side of the leg over the neck.

## Safety
Chokes can cause loss of consciousness in seconds. Tap early, release immediately at the tap, and train under supervision.
